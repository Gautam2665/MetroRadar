One thing I would change from our previous vision

Earlier we called this the AI Gateway.

I think that's too narrow.

I would rename it the Intelligence Gateway.

Why?

Because in the future it won't just route requests to LLMs.

It will also decide:

Should this query even use AI?
Can this be answered directly from the Journey Engine?
Which provider gives the best balance of cost, latency, and quality?
Should the response be streamed?
Should the answer be translated?
Should the watch receive text or synthesized speech?

Most user requests in TransitOS won't actually need an LLM at all.

For example:

"When is the next train?"
"Which platform?"
"What's the fare?"
"Which coach should I board?"

Those should be answered directly from TransitOS APIs in milliseconds.

The LLM is only invoked when it genuinely adds value: natural conversation, multilingual interaction, explanation, summarization, voice, or image understanding.

And I think that's the architectural decision that will make TransitOS scalable. Even if one day you process 10 million transit queries per day, perhaps only 5–10% of them will require an LLM. The remaining 90%+ can be served directly by your own platform, keeping costs low while making TransitOS the true source of intelligence.
