# Line 2B GTFS scope and limitations

- Current operational slice: Mandale, Mankhurd, BSNL Metro, Shivaji Chowk, Diamond Garden, Chembur (six stations).
- Station coordinates and the 123-vertex operational alignment come from the normalized CTM and ArcGIS feature 54.
- Line 2B is a distinct route from Line 2A. The Yellow color family does not create through-running or an Andheri West interchange in this feed.
- No Line 2B trips, stop times, frequencies, platforms, or levels are emitted: the normalized CTM marks schedule/runtime and as-built platform semantics as source-required.
- The planned corridor geometry and DPR station proposals remain in the master evidence/GIS layer; they are not current operational GTFS stops.
- Feed metadata date is a build date, not a claim that the feed contains an official timetable.

Source baseline: MMRDA Line 2B overview/status (updated 31 August 2026) and normalized Line 2B CTM, validated against MoHUA/Esri ArcGIS feature 54.
